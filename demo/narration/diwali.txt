It was Diwali night, and the house was glowing. A little one sat beside Dadi, who smelled like cardamom and warm milk. In Dadi's hands was a tiny clay lamp. Tonight we light lots of diyas, so the whole world feels bright. Long ago, lamps along the path said welcome home. They shared one small laddoo. Then Dadi lit the diya. The flame was gold and sleepy. When the lamp goes quiet, the light stays in your heart. Good night, little one.
