Tip, tip, tip. Rain tapped the window. A little one pressed a nose to the glass. Dadi sat close, shawl soft as a cloud. When lots of rain comes, we call it the monsoon. The sky shares its water with the trees, the frogs, and little ones at home. A frog said tarr tarr. Dadi made warm golden milk with a little honey. What comes after the rain? A rainbow, and then a quiet bed. Good night. I am right here.
