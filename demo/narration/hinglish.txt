Raat ka time ho gaya tha, aur ghar us shaanti mein aa gaya tha jo neend se pehle aati hai.

{child} ke dono pair rug pe samete hue the, aur {narrator} ka shoulder touch ho raha tha. Bahar ek scooter nikal gaya, phir lane bilkul still ho gayi. Window ki sill pe ek chhota clay lamp rakha tha, mutthi jitna, aur uski cotton wick ek soye hue mouse ki tarah mudi hui thi.

"{child}, lamp jalaayein?" {narrator} ne soft voice mein poocha.

{child} ne sir hilaya. Kaam chhota tha, lekin bilkul serious wala.

{narrator} ne match jalayi. Flame pehle sharmaayi. Phir wick ko touch karke gold colour mein khadi ho gayi. Room tez bright nahi hua. Woh kind ho gaya. Corners mein shadows rahi, aur unhone kisi ko bother nahi kiya.

"Yeh lamp poore city ke liye nahi hai," {narrator} ne kaha. "Yeh is room ke liye hai, aur tumhare liye. Jab din loud raha ho, ek chhoti light kaafi hai."

Dono ne saath-saath use dekha. Flame left side ko jhuki, phir right side ko, jaise sun rahi ho.

{child} ne lamp ko din ki baat batai. Ek mango itna sweet tha ki almost zyada tha, aur ek friend ne crayon share kiya tha. Lamp ne kuch fix nahi kiya. Woh bas wahin raha, jaise dost rehta hai jab kehne ko koi clever line na ho.

{narrator} ne thoda milk garam kiya, aur spoon ke quiet hone tak honey ghola. {child} ne dheere-dheere piya. Warmth toes tak pahunchi aur wahin rukne ka mann bana liya.

"Jab hum so jaate hain to moon kya karta hai?" {child} ne poocha.

"Woh lane sambhalta hai," {narrator} ne kaha. "Aur main blanket ke is paar hoon. Aaj raat tumhe kuch nahi sambhalna. Na toys, na worries, na din ka last tukda."

Razai upar aa gayi, soft aur thodi heavy, achhi wali heavy. {narrator} ne use chhoti chin ke neeche dabaya aur ear ke paas ek wrinkle theek ki. Ek haath blanket pe raha, warm aur sure, bina kuch ginne.

"Agar neend khule, to lamp tab bhi friend hoga, aur main yahin hoon," {narrator} ne kaha. "Aankhein band kar sakte ho. Story khud finish ho jayegi."

{child} ne ek aankh band ki, phir doosri. Flame ne ek chhota sound kiya, jaise koi page palat gaya ho. Kahin ek dog step pe settle ho gaya. Kahin fan ghoomta raha, bina hurry ke.

"Good night, {child}," {narrator} ne fusfusaya. "Lamp quiet ho jaaye to bhi light tumhare heart mein rehti hai. Main yahin hoon."

Room thehra raha. Raat narm thi. Aur {child} ki aankhein band ho gayi, us chhoti golden flame ke pehra dete, jab tak morning dheere-dheere aane ko ready na ho.
