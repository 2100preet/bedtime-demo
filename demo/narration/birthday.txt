It was a birthday, and the evening was in no hurry. Dadi opened a window dressed with marigolds. This story is yours. Only yours. They followed a path of paper stars. A quiet elephant made of cloud waited, then bowed. At the table there was one mango slice and one tiny lamp. Make a wish that fits in a whisper. The flame nodded, left, then right. The birthday light stays in your heart when the lamp goes quiet. Happy birthday. Good night.
