Sample story: Aarav and Dadi's Diwali Diya

Questions

What do you collect about my child?

Only a first name, an age range and optional favourites. No photos, no last names, no birthdays, no location. Parents create the account. Children never do.

How does voice consent work?

The grandparent records and approves their own voice with clear, explicit consent. Their voice is used only for your family's stories, is never shared or sold, and can be deleted at any time with one message.

Is it all in Hindi?

It's a gentle mix. Mostly English with simple Hindi words (diya, baarish, Nani) explained naturally in the story, so kids learn as they listen. You can choose more Hindi or more English.

Is this screen time?

No. Stories are audio-first, made for lights-off bedtime. Illustrations are optional.

Ages?

Made for ages 2–6.
